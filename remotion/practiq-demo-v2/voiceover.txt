Practiq is the AI ops layer for boutique professional firms shipping redlined Word memos to clients.

Drop in your draft Word doc plus the firm's prior memos for that client. Practiq reads what's been said before, cross-checking phrasing and structure across the firm's voice, not a generic template.

Back comes a Word doc with native tracked changes. Open in Word. Accept or reject inside Word. Every change cites which prior memo it pulled the rationale from.
